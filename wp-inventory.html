Network & Server Closet

The core of your network: internet, firewall, servers, and backups.

  • ISP modem / fiber ONT

    This is where the internet enters your building, and when it goes down, everything stops. We watch your circuit around the clock and deal with the internet provider for you, so nobody on your team sits on hold. Where it makes sense, we add a backup connection that takes over automatically.

    • 24/7 circuit monitoring with outage alerts
    • We open and chase tickets with your ISP (internet service provider)
    • Automatic failover to a second connection
    • Speed and uptime checks against what you pay for

    Network management

  • Firewall

    Your firewall decides what is allowed in and out of the office network. We run it as a next-generation firewall (NGFW) that inspects traffic for threats and blocks known-bad sites. Firmware patches happen on a schedule, not whenever someone remembers.

    • Next-generation firewall setup and rule management
    • Threat, malware, and web filtering
    • Firmware patching on a managed schedule
    • Secure VPN (virtual private network) access for remote staff
    • Logs reviewed 24/7 by the CMIT SOC

    Cybersecurity services

  • Core switch + patch panel

    Every wired device in the office plugs in here. We split the network into VLANs (virtual LANs, separate lanes for different kinds of traffic) and lock down unused ports. Every cable gets documented, so troubleshooting takes minutes instead of an afternoon.

    • VLAN design and network segmentation
    • Port security and unused-port shutdown
    • Labeled, documented cabling
    • Firmware updates and health monitoring

    Network management

  • Server Rack

    The rack is the physical home of your most important equipment. We plan the layout, keep airflow and cooling in check, and label everything. The result is a closet that is safe to work in and easy to hand off.

    • Rack layout and cable management
    • Cooling and temperature monitoring
    • Clear labeling and as-built documentation
